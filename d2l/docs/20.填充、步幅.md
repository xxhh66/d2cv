## 填充



![image-20260705160335341](assets/image-20260705160335341.png)



![image-20260705160408236](assets/image-20260705160408236.png)


$$
(n_h - k_h + 1) \times (n_w - k_w + 1)
$$
四周填充零，填充后在进行卷积计算.

![image-20260705160656409](assets/image-20260705160656409.png)

填充大小，通常取核宽高减1

$p_{h} = k_{h}-1$

$p_{w} = k_{w}-1$

$k_{h}=3$
$p_{h} =2$
左右填充为1

![image-20260705160822710](assets/image-20260705160822710.png)
$$
(n_h - k_h+p_h + 1) \times (n_w - k_w + p_w+ 1)
$$
